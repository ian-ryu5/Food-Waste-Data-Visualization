# Food-Gooder

**An ultimate data visualization tool for food utilization in schools**

*Save Food. Save Us & Earth.*

## Overview

Food-Gooder is a data visualization project that illustrates the scale of food waste in U.S. K-12 schools and proposes a collaborative framework for reducing it. Schools serve around 3 million lunches per school day nationwide, and national food waste in schools amounts to an estimated 530,000 tons per school year. This project translates those abstract numbers into visuals people can intuitively grasp.

## The Data, By the Numbers

| Category | Impact |
|---|---|
| Environment | 11 million gallons of water wasted per day |
| Economy | $9.7 million (US) wasted per day |
| Support | 9 million kids facing hunger in the US |

To make these numbers tangible, the project reframes them as everyday comparisons:

- **11 million gallons/day** ≈ the volume of water that would fill a football field to a depth of 0.6 yards
- **55 million gallons/week** — the daily total shown stacked across a five-day school week
- **530,000 tons/year of food waste** ≈ the weight of over 100,000 cars, or **555 cars' worth of food wasted per day** (2,775 cars/week)
- **9 million hungry kids** — visualized at a scale referencing NYC's population of 8.7 million to underscore the size of the problem

## The Collaboration Model

The project frames waste reduction as a shared responsibility across four levels, each paired with a driving goal:

| Level | Goal | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Kids | Awareness | Help students understand how food waste affects their lives and the planet |
| Schools | Motivation | Empower individuals with ways to reduce food waste |
| District / Community | Connectivity | Use collective intelligence and networks to redistribute food |
| USA / World | Influence | Frame food waste as a serious global issue to solve together |

## Food Repurposing Flow

Schools, communities, and facilities form a simple donation loop:

**School** (donates and repurposes food) → **Community** (creates distribution channels) → **Facility** (receives and utilizes food)

## Design Symbol: "Grow Sprout and Save Earth"

A sprout-in-a-circle icon represents the gauge of food utilization by participants. Communities can assign their own color palette to the icon to indicate activity level, encouraging participants to work toward their food-utilization goals.

## Design Challenge Questions

### 1. Tell us about the information you're visualizing.

The data presented is the amount of food being wasted, scaled from a local community up to the entire United States and the world. The idea started after joining a non-profit orchestra that regularly ran food fundraisers — food waste turned out to be the biggest reason behind them. A health teacher also confirmed that the school itself was wasting significant food, which made clear that this is a serious, widespread problem worth surfacing with real numbers. Sharing this data matters because in countries with less stable governments and resources, people don't have the same access to food or clean water. Reducing food waste at the community level can save money and, ultimately, save lives. The people most affected by this information are the ones doing the wasting — the goal is to show them the reality of just how much is being lost.

### 3. Tell us about your design.

The design shows how large-scale food waste really is by scaling the visualization from a small community up to a larger one — a small-to-large circle progression meant to make the numbers feel as overwhelming visually as they are in reality. Beyond the visuals, the goal is to challenge viewers to think about how their own communities relate to food waste, track their own habits, and work toward wasting less — ultimately framing it as everyone doing their part to "save enough food for the world."
